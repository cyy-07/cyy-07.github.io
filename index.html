About Me关于我

I’m Yiyang Chen, a Chemical Engineering graduate from East China University of Science and Technology (ECUST). During my senior year, I completed a one-year exchange program at UC Riverside, with additional academic training in Computer Science and AI. After graduating, I stayed at UCR as a student intern with Professional and Global Education. I developed the knowledge base and prompting workflows for a multilingual NotebookLM academic adviser integrated into UCR’s website.我是陈祎洋，毕业于华东理工大学（ECUST）化学工程专业。大四期间，我在加州大学河滨分校参加为期一年的交换项目，并接受计算机科学与人工智能方面的额外学术训练。毕业后，我留在 UCR 的 Professional and Global Education 担任学生实习生，为整合进 UCR 网站的多语言 NotebookLM 学业咨询助手开发知识库并设计提示词工作流。

I develop machine learning methods for molecular systems and industrial processes. Under Professor Jianzhong Wu’s supervision, I designed a deterministic exact-cover assignment that partitions each molecule into nonoverlapping functional groups. I built separate four-layer GIN encoders for solute and solvent group graphs, with an Apelblat readout for temperature-dependent solubility. Across 182,608 measurements and five held-out solute–solvent splits, the model achieved an MAE of 0.313 ± 0.013 log units. At ECUST, I developed a multi-objective scheduling algorithm that incorporates furnace-specific coke accumulation into an ε-constraint MINLP for eight ethylene cracking furnaces. Relative to the maximum-profit schedule evaluated, the minimum-emission schedule reduced daily CO₂ by 2.69% with a 1.86% reduction in profit. I enjoy hackathons, where I use Next.js and TypeScript to build AI applications such as FluxMind, a student workload planner powered by Gemini. I also built the Three.js travel explorer TerraPulse, which received the Innovative Product Ideas award at DesignVerse 2026.我的研究涉及分子系统与工业过程中的机器学习方法。在吴建中教授指导下，我设计了确定性的精确覆盖分配算法，将每个分子划分为互不重叠的官能团；并分别为溶质与溶剂的官能团图构建四层 GIN 编码器，通过 Apelblat 读出层预测温度相关的溶解度。模型使用 182,608 条实验数据，在五组未见过的溶质-溶剂划分上取得 0.313 ± 0.013 log units 的 MAE。在华东理工大学，我开发了考虑各炉结焦状态的多目标调度算法，通过 ε 约束 MINLP 协调八台乙烯裂解炉的运行。在已评估的调度中，相比利润最高的方案，排放最低方案的日 CO₂ 排放减少 2.69%，对应利润减少 1.86%。我喜欢参加黑客松，用 Next.js 和 TypeScript 开发 AI 应用，例如由 Gemini 驱动的学生任务规划工具 FluxMind。我还开发了基于 Three.js 的旅行探索应用 TerraPulse，并在 DesignVerse 2026 获得 Innovative Product Ideas 奖。

Outside research, I like hiking (hence the tent button 🏕), swimming, stargazing, and occasionally reminding myself that rest is productive. I’m applying to MS and PhD programs in Computer Science for Fall 2027, with a focus on developing machine learning algorithms for molecular design and drug discovery (AI for Science).科研之外，我喜欢徒步（所以有了那个搭帐篷按钮 🏕）、游泳和观星，偶尔提醒自己休息也是生产力。目前我正在申请 2027 年秋季入学的计算机科学硕士及博士项目，研究重点为开发面向分子设计与药物发现的机器学习算法（AI for Science）。
