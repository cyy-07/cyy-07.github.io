Thermodynamics-Guided Functional Group Graph Neural Network for Molecular Solubility Prediction热力学引导的官能团图神经网络用于分子溶解度预测2025 – Present2025年 – 至今
Under Professor Jianzhong Wu’s supervision · UC Riverside导师 吴建中教授 · 加州大学河滨分校
View ToC查看 ToC

I developed a deterministic exact cover assignment that partitions each molecule into nonoverlapping functional groups. Separate four-layer GIN encoders process solute and solvent group graphs, and an Apelblat readout predicts temperature-dependent solubility. The study uses 182,608 measurements. Across five held-out solute–solvent pair splits, MAE is 0.313 ± 0.013 log units. Solvent-conditioned group terms are examined against established chemical trends.我开发了确定性的 exact cover 分组方法，将分子划分为互不重叠的官能团。溶质和溶剂的官能团图分别经四层 GIN 编码，再通过 Apelblat 读出预测随温度变化的溶解度。研究采用 182,608 条测量记录。在五次未见溶质–溶剂配对划分中，MAE 为 0.313 ± 0.013 log 单位，并将溶剂相关的官能团项与已有化学趋势进行比较。

Advisors · Prof. Wenli Du & Prof. Liang Zhao · ECUST导师 杜文莉教授与赵亮教授 · 华东理工大学
View Figure 1 · System architecture查看图 1 · 系统架构

I model furnace-specific coke accumulation and operating-age responses, and solve single-furnace operating optimization and MINLP scheduling for eight heterogeneous furnaces with the ε-constraint method. For the same 368 day schedule in Table 1, clean-state estimates overpredict daily profit by 1.10% and underpredict daily CO₂ by 1.26%. Across the evaluated cluster endpoints, daily CO₂ decreases by 2.692% with a 1.859% profit reduction. Ethylene production decreases more than emissions in that comparison, so CO₂ intensity increases.我对各裂解炉的结焦累积与运行时间相关响应建模，并采用 ε 约束法求解单炉运行优化与八台异构裂解炉的 MINLP 调度。在 Table 1 的同一套 368 天调度下，洁净状态估计高估日利润 1.10%，低估日 CO₂ 排放 1.26%。在所评估的炉群端点之间，日 CO₂ 排放下降 2.692%，日利润下降 1.859%。该比较中乙烯产量的降幅大于排放降幅，因此 CO₂ 排放强度上升。
